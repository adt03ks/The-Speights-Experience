Realtor Rewards
A Little Something Extra

The Riceland Realtor Rewards program is designed to thank agents for connecting families with a place they’ll love calling home.

The more homes you help close in Riceland, the greater your bonus per closing.

✦   Realtor Bonus Details
Incentive Overview

A Closing Bonus That Grows With Your Sales

Beginning August 1, 2026, each closing through December 31, 2026 can qualify participating real estate professionals for a Realtor Rewards bonus in Riceland.

According to the flyer, the more homes sold by the end of the year, the bigger the per-closing reward.

Bonus Structure

Realtor Rewards by Closing Count

The flyer lists three reward levels based on the number of closings completed during the program period.

Reward Tier 01
$250 per closing

1st, 2nd & 3rd Sales

The first three qualified closings are eligible for a $250 reward per closing.

Reward Tier 02
$500 per closing

4th & 5th Sales

The fourth and fifth qualified closings are eligible for a $500 reward per closing.

Reward Tier 03
$1,000 per closing

6th & Future Sales

Beginning with the sixth qualified closing, the reward increases to $1,000 per closing.

Program Dates & Key Details

  • Program start: August 1, 2026
  • Program end: December 31, 2026
  • Bonuses are based on closings completed during the listed promotion period.
  • Bonus amounts increase as your qualifying sales count increases.
  • Terms, documentation requirements, and official program rules should be confirmed before relying on the reward.
